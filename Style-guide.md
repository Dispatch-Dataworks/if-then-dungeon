
# If Then Dungeon - Web Style Guide

## This guide defines the visual language, color palette, and textural elements derived from the game's artwork to ensure a cohesive website design. The overall aesthetic is a dark, magical fantasy dungeon with glowing runic technology.
1. General Aesthetics & Mood
 * Theme: Dark Fantasy, Dungeon Crawler, Magic-Tech, Adventure.
 * Atmosphere: Mysterious, ancient, glowing, and slightly perilous. The lighting is dramatic, primarily from magical runes and fire.
 * Textures:
   * Stone: Rough, cracked, ancient stone blocks with deep shadows.
   * Magic/Runes: Glowing, neon energy that appears etched into stone or flowing through conduits.
   * Paper/Parchment: Aged, worn, slightly burned edges, used for borders and UI panels.
   * Leather & Metal: Worn leather armor and dark, forged metal with glowing accents.
2. Color Palette
The color scheme is dominated by dark, cool tones with bright, glowing contrasts.
Primary Backgrounds & Surfaces
 * Dark Stone / Deep Shadow: #0A0A14 to #1A1A2E (Very dark blue-black)
 * Mid-Tone Stone: #2C3146 (Desaturated dark blue-gray)
 * Aged Parchment (Borders/UI): #CDB38B to #8B6F47 (Warm, brownish-gold)
Glowing Accents & Magic
 * Primary Rune Glow (Cyan/Blue): #00F0FF to #0066FF (Electric cyan to deep blue)
 * Secondary Magic Glow (Purple): #9D00FF (Vibrant purple, used for some runes and gears)
UI & Interactive Elements
 * "True" / Success (Green): #00FF66 (Bright, neon green)
 * "False" / Danger (Red/Orange): #FF3300 to #FF6600 (Fiery red-orange)
 * Fire / Torchlight (Warm Yellow/Orange): #FFD700 to #FF8C00 (Golden yellow to deep orange)
3. Typography & Iconography
 * Logo Font Style: A custom, runic, chiseled stone style with a magical blue glow. For the web, use a similar decorative fantasy font for headings (e.g., a Google Font like "Cinzel Decorative" or "MedievalSharp").
 * Body Font Style: A clean, legible sans-serif or a slightly stylized serif font that complements the fantasy theme without being hard to read (e.g., "Montserrat" or "Lora").
 * Icons: All icons should have a glowing, runic, or etched appearance. Buttons for "True" and "False" should use the distinct green checkmark and red 'X' from the artwork, enclosed in a glowing circle.
4. UI Element Styling
 * Containers & Panels: Use the aged parchment texture for main content areas or modal windows, with the torn/burned edge effect. Alternatively, use dark stone panels with glowing blue borders.
 * Buttons:
   * Primary Call-to-Action: Give them a glowing runic border (cyan or gold) with a dark stone background, making them appear to pulse with energy on hover.
   * Logic Buttons (True/False): Replicate the circular, glowing style from the art.
 * Borders & Dividers: Use glowing blue lines, gear patterns, or the parchment edge texture to separate sections.
